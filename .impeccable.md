## Design Context

### Users
German-speaking women (25–55), often buying as gifts or for their own home. They browse on their phones in the evening, at home, relaxed. They value quality, authenticity, and the story behind a product. They're suspicious of "corporate" and attracted to "personal."

### Brand Personality
Warm, maternal, artisanal — like Mama's Küche. Not a trendy DTC startup. Not a luxury spa. Think: the label on a homemade jar of jam, a wooden kitchen sign, handwritten recipe cards. The German word "gemütlich" — cozy, personal, unpretentious — is the core.

**3-word personality**: warm · handmade · intimate

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme**: Light — these candles are bought on Sunday mornings and gifted with love
- **Typography**: Vollkorn (display, headings — bread-like, warm, deeply Germanic serif) + Jost (body, UI — clean geometric sans)
- **Colors**: Terracotta flame accent (oklch ~54% 0.13 34deg) on warm cream surfaces. All neutrals tinted toward amber-brown hue. No cold grays.
- **Layout**: Left-aligned where possible, asymmetric composition, generous breathing room. NOT centered-everything.
- **Anti-references**: no luxury spa sterility, no generic Shopify template, no startup-cool dark mode, no Pinterest moodboard aesthetic

### Design Principles
1. **Personal over perfect** — feels made by a person, not optimized by an algorithm
2. **Warmth in every detail** — color, font choice, and spacing should all feel like a warm kitchen, not a showroom
3. **Typography does the work** — Vollkorn italic for display moments creates personality without illustration
4. **Breathing room** — generous whitespace signals confidence and calm, not emptiness
5. **One voice** — the entire site should feel like one coherent handwritten letter, not assembled from parts
